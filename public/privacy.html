Privacy Policy

PBL — Pixel Basketball League ("PBL", "the game", "we"). Last updated: November 5, 2026.

PBL is a basketball franchise management game available at https://pbl-online.onrender.com and as an Android app that opens that website. This policy explains what information the game uses and what we do with it.

Information we collect

  • Username. If you play online, you choose a username (3–16 letters, numbers or underscores). It is shown to other players in rooms, on leaderboards and on the trade board. Please do not use your real name or personal information as a username.
  • Sign-in token. When you create a username, the server creates a random token that proves the username is yours. It is stored on your device and on our server. There are no passwords and no email addresses collected by the game.
  • Game and ranking data. Your online rating, wins and losses (all-time and monthly).
  • Cloud saves. If you use cloud saves, we store your franchise save data (team, rosters, season progress, achievements) so you can load it later.
  • Trade board listings. If you list a player on the world trade board, we store that player's details, an optional note you write, your username and the time.
  • Room messages. While you are in a Co-op or Fight room, game data is relayed between players in that room in real time. It is not kept after the room closes.
  • On your device. The game stores local saves, settings and achievements in your browser or app storage (for example localStorage). This stays on your device unless you use cloud saves.

Information we do not collect

  • No real names, email addresses, phone numbers or payment information through the game.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos or microphone/camera data.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.

How we use information

Only to run the game: to sign you in, show leaderboards, run rooms and the trade board, and store your saves. We do not sell your information and we do not share it with advertisers.

Where information is stored

The game's server is hosted on Render (render.com), and account and save data may be stored in a hosted database service. These providers process data on our behalf to run the game. Data may be stored in the United States or other countries where those providers operate.

Public information

Your username, rating, wins and losses appear on public leaderboards. Your username and the player you list appear on the trade board for other players to see.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account, ranking and cloud saves while the game is running. You can delete your cloud saves inside the game, and you can clear local data by clearing your browser or app storage. To have your username and all associated data deleted, email us at the address below from the contact details you want removed (include your username) and we will delete it.

Children

PBL is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because usernames and a note on the trade board are visible to other players, we ask that children play with a parent's permission. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Connections to the game use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we use reasonable measures to protect the data we store.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
